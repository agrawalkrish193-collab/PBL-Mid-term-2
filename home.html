Intelligent Wardrobe Recommendation System

A personalized AI stylist integrating Computer Vision, NLP, and Compatibility Modeling to generate context-aware and sustainable outfit recommendations from the user's existing wardrobe.

Problem Statement

Existing fashion recommendation systems treat clothing as static items and rely heavily on purchase history. They fail to incorporate dynamic context such as weather, occasion, mood, and personal style preferences. Additionally, most systems promote new purchases rather than optimizing existing wardrobe utilization. Our system acts as an AI stylist that intelligently assembles harmonious outfits using ML and NLP.

Literature Review

Recent academic work has focused heavily on learning visual representations (embeddings) of clothing items to determine compatibility within an outfit. Methods like Joint Embedding Networks and Siamese Networks are used to measure the "fit" score between two or more items.

Research Gap & Innovation

Integrating real-time data like weather, event type, and social media trends for even more relevant suggestions. Leveraging augmented reality for immersive virtual try-on experiences. Exploring generative models to create unique clothing designs based on user preferences and style evolution.

System Methodology

Dataset

• DeepFashion – 800K images, rich bounding boxes, attribute annotations, consumer-to-shop matching.

• Fashion MNIST – 70K images of 10 clothing categories (black & white thumbnails).

• WGSN Trend Data – High-level trend forecasts, color palettes, stylistic concepts.

Model / Architecture

• BERT for understanding complex contextual requests.

• YOLO / ResNet for clothing recognition, classification & attribute extraction.

• Siamese Networks for learning outfit compatibility.
